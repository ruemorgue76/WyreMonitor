What WyreMonitor installs

WyreMonitor reads your PC's temperatures, fans and loads. To do this, it installs:

- The WyreMonitor app, in C:\Program Files\WyreMonitor. It runs with your normal rights.

- The WyreMonitor service. It runs in the background with system rights, starts with Windows,
  and only reads sensors. It serves the readings to the app on this PC (127.0.0.1) and cannot
  be reached from other computers.

- The PawnIO driver, if it isn't already installed. CPU temperatures and fan speeds can only be
  read through a driver. The installer downloads PawnIO 2.2.0 from its official GitHub page
  (github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Setup), checks the file's SHA-256, then installs it. PawnIO is
  signed by its author and works with Windows Memory Integrity turned on.

Nothing is sent from your PC. The only internet requests are the PawnIO download during this
installation and, only when you ask for it, the check for a new WyreMonitor version on GitHub.

To remove WyreMonitor: Windows Settings > Apps > WyreMonitor > Uninstall. This removes the app,
the service and their settings. PawnIO stays, because other programs may use it; you can
uninstall it the same way.
