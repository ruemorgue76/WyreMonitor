**Cleaner updates and uninstall**

- Installing a new version over an older one now replaces every program file: files an older version left in its folder are removed, and your settings are kept.
- The update waits until the WyreMonitor service has really stopped before copying files, and the service can no longer restart by itself in the middle of the update.
- If an update is cancelled or fails halfway, the previous service is started again.
- Uninstalling now removes everything: the program folder, the service, the sign-in entry and your WyreMonitor settings. Only the PawnIO driver stays, because other programs may use it.
